## Scams
[F04] {scam, digital arrest} Digital arrest is a scam. No police officer or agency arrests anyone, or demands money, over a phone or video call. | headline: Digital arrest is a SCAM | visual: phone_warning | source: MHA / I4C public advisory on digital arrest scams | page: 1
[F05] {scam, pressure} Scammers create fear, urgency and secrecy. Pause, hang up, and talk to someone you trust. | headline: Pause. Hang up. Talk. | visual: phone_call | source: I4C public awareness advisory on cyber frauds | page: 1
[F06] {upi, otp, payment} Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or card details. You never need a PIN to receive money. | headline: Never share OTP or PIN | visual: rupee_lock | source: RBI / NPCI public awareness advisory on digital payment fraud | page: 1
[F07] {job, fee} Genuine employers do not ask for registration fees or security deposits for a job. | headline: Real jobs never ask for fees | visual: briefcase | source: I4C public awareness advisory on fake job offers | page: 1

## Sextortion and online harassment
[F08] {sextortion, harassment} If someone threatens you online, do not pay. Save the chats, block the account and report it. It is not your fault. | headline: Not your fault. Don't pay. | visual: chat_shield | source: I4C public awareness advisory on sextortion | page: 1
